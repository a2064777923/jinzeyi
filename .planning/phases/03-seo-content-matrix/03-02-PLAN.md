<threat_model>
| Threat | Severity | Mitigation |
|--------|----------|------------|
| Invalid scene/year params cause runtime errors or expensive computation | high | Validate scene slug against typed registry and year with `isLegalRouteYear`; call `notFound()` for invalid values. |
| Route expansion accidentally generates 5001 years per scene | high | `generateStaticParams` uses `getIndexedYearRange`, not legal dynamic range. |
| Ominous dates are hidden, creating misleading results | medium | Result status carries recommended/caution/not-preferred and UI renders caution rows visibly. |
| Filter state creates crawl traps | medium | Filters are client/local controls or query params excluded from sitemap; canonical remains scene-year route. |
</threat_model>
