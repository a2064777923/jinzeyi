| Threat | Severity | Mitigation |
|--------|----------|------------|
| Unbounded year params trigger expensive computation or build-time route explosion | high | `src/lib/almanac/year-support.ts` separates legal dynamic range 0-5000 from indexed range 2006-2046 and tests both. |
| JSON-LD injection through content strings | medium | JSON-LD helpers accept typed static data and serialize with `JSON.stringify`; content remains local TS data. |
| Thin-content or duplicate-content pages harm SEO quality | medium | Registry tests require unique slug, title, description, main copy, FAQ, and related links for every indexable page. |
| Locale alternates point to non-existent routes | medium | Metadata and sitemap helpers consume the same registry and locale helper outputs. |
</threat_model>
